## Deviations from Plan

### Review follow-up fix

**1. [Concurrency bug] `path_conflict` could announce adoption without inserting the row**
- **Found during:** post-execution review
- **Issue:** when a concurrent winner uploaded the object but its insert failed, the loser received `path_conflict`, classified `reused/adoption:true`, and returned without inserting the row.
- **Fix:** `path_conflict` and `unique_violation` now pass an adoption decision through `adoptOrReclassify`, which inserts the row, handles `23505` by re-reading/reclassifying, and never announces `created`/`reused` before persistence is confirmed.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/campaign/composition-artifacts/persist.ts`, `__tests__/persist.contract.test.ts`
- **Verification:** controlled concurrency test (winner uploads, insert fails; second call gets conflict and adopts with exactly one row).
- **Committed in:** `f86a4de3`

**2. [False success] conflict re-read could return `created` with nothing persisted**
- **Found during:** post-execution review
- **Issue:** after a conflict, if the re-read found no row and no object, `decidePersistOutcome` returned `created` and it escaped as success. The same leak existed in the `23505` branches.
- **Fix:** `settleConflictResult` now converts any post-conflict `created` into `persistence_failure` with `reason: "conflict_without_persisted_artifact"`. Both `path_conflict` and `unique_violation` route through it, and `adoptOrReclassify` rejects a reclassified `created`. `reused` only escapes with a validated row/object or after a confirmed adoption insert.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/campaign/composition-artifacts/persist.ts`, `__tests__/persist.contract.test.ts`
- **Verification:** negative test where the first call persists, rows are cleared, the object is hidden once and removed on the next path conflict; the result is `persistence_failure`/`conflict_without_persisted_artifact`, with zero rows, zero objects and no removal.
- **Committed in:** `ddc578d2`

## Deviations from Plan

None that change behavior or scope. Two literal-wording alignments are recorded:

1. **Orphan adoption requires JPEG (not PNG/WebP bytes).**
   - The Task 1 action text mentions “exigir JPEG/PNG/WebP 1024×1024”, but the design (`D4`), spec scenario (“JPEG 1024×1024”) and F60.1 `ROLE_POLICY.square_original` all require JPEG. Accepting PNG/WebP bytes would make `persistCompositionArtifact` return `integrity_failure` instead of a gate conflict. The gate therefore decodes with Sharp and requires real JPEG 1024×1024; provider conversion still accepts PNG/JPEG/WebP before `resizeToJpeg1024`.
2. **Exact blocked reason for `unproven`/`corrupt`.**
   - The plan requires `blocked` without naming the reason. Implemented as `artifact_without_frozen_state` (no provable original content) for `absent`/`unproven`/`corrupt`, and `pre_call_probe_indeterminate` for `indeterminate`. This is documented in `decideReuseAdmissibility` and its matrix test.

No bug/security/blocking deviation, no package install, no dependency added.

- The transport/artifact modules are not wired into a public orchestrator yet; `run-square-base.ts`, copy, telemetry and boundary/regression suites belong to 60.4-03/04.
